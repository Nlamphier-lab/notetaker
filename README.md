# Notetaker

**Notetaker** is a smart lecture assistant that uses your laptop's microphone to capture what your professor is saying, display live captions, and automatically generate structured study notes highlighting the most important concepts in real time.

---

## Features

- **Live Speech-to-Text Captions:** Captures audio directly from your laptop microphone and displays real-time captions during lectures.
- **Saved Lecture Transcripts:** Automatically saves full session transcripts so you can review or search through what was said later.
- **AI-Generated Smart Notes:** A dedicated side panel analyzes the incoming captions to extract key points, definitions, and takeaways—filtering out filler so you get actual notes rather than a raw wall of text.
- **Session History:** Organize and revisit past lectures with both the raw transcript and synthesized notes side-by-side.

---

## How It Works

1. **Start a Session:** Grant microphone permissions and hit record when class begins.
2. **Follow Along Live:** Watch the live captions populate on one side of the screen as your professor speaks.
3. **Review Smart Highlights:** As the lecture progresses, the notes panel distills the captions into bulleted key concepts and important takeaways.
4. **Save & Study:** End the session to save both the full transcript and the condensed notes for future review.
